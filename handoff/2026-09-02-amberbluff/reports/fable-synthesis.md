# Fable synthesis: settling MAX_11 at two hidden layers

Author: Fable 5.1 (Claude lineage), fresh context, 2026-09-02. Inputs: cluster1-6, lit-scout, lower-bound, wang-basu reports; probe9p.log and probe10p.log; the lead's n=5..8 results; STATUS, OPEN_GAPS, CHARTER. Every number is cited to its source. Anything marked *speculative* is mine. The lead's n=10 probe and a second-prime n=9 run were still executing when I wrote this, so r(10) is not yet available.

## 1. Where the problem actually stands

**Fact 1. The live question is one finite linear-algebra problem whose effective dimension is small, and nobody has ever run it.** Inside the Rueß ansatz the n=11, k=5 system is 657,833 rows by 12,179,657 columns (wang-basu.md, Burnside and primitive-ray counts, matching the campaign's own numbers). The loop-free simple-pair subfamily, which contains every one of the 337 and 402 upstream MAX9 and MAX10 terms, has 462,627 templates (lead's Burnside). The decisive datum is the rank: 8, 13, 90, 140, 1,506 for n=5..9 against 19, 25, 357, 430, 10,976 columns (lead's message; probe9p.log). Per k-step the rank grew 11x and 17x while columns grew 19x, 31x, 42x. *Speculative extrapolation:* r(11) is 30k-50k, with 90k as a pessimistic ceiling. So the span of symmetrized atoms is a roughly 40k-dimensional space of symmetric CPWL functions sitting inside a hinge space I estimate at 100k-250k directions (the subset of the 657,822-direction universe with zero first coordinate, since a loop-free atom never sees the minimum). The 12M-column framing intimidated everyone, but the true object is a rank-40k matrix. Every campaign negative (9,804 same-component classes, 8,107 Y-spokes, 26,689 with closure, 163,740 MAX10-lifts) was on a subfamily chosen for convenience rather than canonicity; cluster5 notes the complete 163,740-column question could have been decided in 2-3 CPU-days instead of a sixty-iteration treadmill.

**Fact 2. Positive and negative outcomes are radically asymmetric.** A rational certificate in any finite subfamily is a real network, and within a linear ansatz real solvability equals rational solvability (lower-bound.md F3). A negative on any finite family, including the complete k=5 loop-inclusive universe, proves nothing about MAX_11 in ReLU_2. Both construction papers say so (lit-scout section 7), and the campaign proved it concretely: G-0084 Theorem 5 exhibits a degree-6 pair-max function outside the degree-5 orbit space, G-0110 exhibits a single real-generator atom outside the 26,689-column span, and G-0089 shows a nonzero invariant two-hidden-layer function can vanish on any finite set of conic neighbourhoods (cluster3, cluster4). Bakaev's subset-zeta construction matches MAX_n on the whole cube with a bias-free two-layer network (cluster2 section 6), so no finite-evaluation invariant can ever refute. Computation can settle the rung only in the positive direction. Refutation is a new theorem.

**Fact 3. Every published lower-bound tool dies at the same hypothesis, and what survives constrains the shape of a witness rather than its existence.** Volume parity needs lattice vertices, mod-p volume needs a common denominator, the braid bound needs conformal breakpoints that the n<=10 second layers already violate, sparse-maxout dimension bounds need fan-in limits, and the stable log_2 bound of Bakaev-Brunck-Yehudayoff is for the unsigned model (lower-bound.md A1-A6; lit-scout section 6). Survivors over R: Koutschan's zero-summand bound, which gives k>=5 and not depth; Safran's width bound, sharpened in lower-bound.md section B to 25 first-layer neurons whose supports 3-cover all 165 triples; and for rational weights, 6 divides the denominator (lower-bound.md section D). Structurally a refutation is an infinite-dimensional dual certificate: a functional on the real polytope algebra killing log[conv(Z u Z')] for all real zonotope pairs but not log[Delta_10]. Continuous Minkowski-additive valuations are integrals of support functions and cannot obstruct signed combinations; discreteness needs integrality; positivity is the ICNN world. Nobody has a candidate, and the campaign never produced one that survived its own G-0110.

**Fact 4. Certificates are arbitrary points of enormous affine spaces, so the structure lives in the span, not in any certificate.** Nullity is 9,470 at n=9 (probe9p.log); the 163,740-column system had nullity about 163,391 at 540 rows (cluster5). That is why every coefficient-law hunt failed (G-0099 leaf induction, G-0114, G-0115; cluster4) and why refuting one basic solution per CEGIS iteration was information-free. The theorem behind six consecutive successes at k = k_min must be a statement about the left kernel, the dual constraints every atom satisfies, or an explicit n-uniform construction. One exact lens, my formulation and elementary: on the sorted cone a loop-free atom never sees the minimum coordinate, and placing vertex v at the minimum turns its incident edges into loops at their other endpoints. Hence every loop-free n=11 column equals the sum over the eleven labels v of the collapsed loop-inclusive n=10 column, in shared 10-position hinge coordinates, and MAX_11 and MAX_10 are the same target vector there. So span(loop-free, n=11, k=5) is the image of an explicit linear vertex-collapse map from the n=10 loop-inclusive k=5 family, whose span already contains the target. This is the representation-independent version of the leaf induction that G-0103 killed; it is the right object for an all-n theorem and a genuine constraint on where a witness can live.

## 2. Ranked paths to settle n=11

**A. Decide the complete loop-free k=5 family exactly. Rank 1.**
Mechanism. (i) Port the lead's per-column normal-form DP to Rust or C++. The Python kernel costs 1.6-6 s per n=11 column (lead's message); a compiled subset-DP should cost 0.05-0.3 s, so 462,627 columns take 1-10 hours on 16 cores. (ii) Never materialize the full matrix: as columns stream, apply two independent sparse random row sketches (each hinge row to 4-8 of m sketch rows, m about 64k), which preserve rank w.h.p. over F_p once m exceeds the rank; agreement of the two sketch ranks, and non-saturation (rank < m), are the checks. (iii) Blocked modular elimination with FFLAS-FFPACK: batches of 4,096 sketched columns reduced by GEMM against a dense echelon basis of size m by r (about 10 GB at r=40k), roughly 4-8 hours total. (iv) If rank([SA|Sb]) = rank(SA) at two primes, solve the r by r pivot system and Dixon-lift (G-0011 did 5,269 squared in 202 s, cluster1; 40k squared is hours and 13 GB), then verify the integer-cleared certificate exactly on all rows by recomputing the r pivot columns in 64-bit integers and accumulating in big rationals. (v) If not, pull a left-kernel vector u of SA back to the dual uS on original rows and Dixon-lift a rational dual exactly as G-0011 did. Gate the pipeline on n=9 and n=10 first (known members; the lead's JSONL for n=9 exists). Bonus: n=12 at k=5 has only 490,480 loop-free templates (my Burnside), so the same run decides the next rung nearly for free.
Cost: 4-6 days engineering, 1-2 days compute, about 40 GB disk if mod-p columns are cached; no rented node unless rank exceeds about 90k, in which case rent one 512 GB node for the elimination only.
Kill condition: rank saturates the sketch at m=96k, or per-column cost stays above 2 s compiled.
P(a) = 0.80. P(b): a positive settles the rung outright and I put P(positive) at 0.55; a negative is a Q-theorem on the first canonical family and settles the family, not the rung.

**B. Extend A to the loopless multiset family (754,017 signed-W classes plus carriers) and then the loop-inclusive 7,015,841-record universe. Rank 2.**
Mechanism: same pipeline; the signed-W collapse (G-0027, G-0028; cluster2) already quotients common edges into the carriers 5E and 5L. Loopless multisets cost about 1.6x of A, loop-inclusive about 15x. Loop-bearing columns carry hinges with nonzero first coordinate (cluster6); finishing the STAR quarantine theorem would let one drop them, but that program stopped with 8 undecided mass-4 directions and 435 untouched mass-5 ones, so computing is cheaper than proving.
Cost: loopless multiset adds 2 days locally; full loop-inclusive needs a 64-core node with at least 1 TB for about 2 days if the rank reaches 200k, otherwise a week locally.
Kill: basis memory.
P(a) = 0.70 loopless, 0.40 loop-inclusive within the window. P(b): conditional on A negative, P(loopless multiset positive) about 0.12; P(loop-inclusive positive | both negative) about 0.15.

**C. Residual-guided enlargement beyond k=5. Rank 3, conditional on A and B negative.**
Mechanism: A's echelon form yields the exact left kernel, and a rational dual's pairing pattern says which hinge directions MAX_11 misses (G-0078's dual concentrated on four-valued inputs; G-0084's escape had signed mass six; cluster3). Price parametric atoms against it: degree-6 loop-free templates, 18,436,223 by my Burnside, 40x of A, and real-weighted graphical zonotopes sum_e c_e [0, e_i - e_j], the Z_t continuum of G-0043 (cluster2), searching for nonzero-price atoms whose new hinges cancel in pairs. Degree 6 is exactly the move Wang-Basu needed at n=8 (wang-basu.md).
Cost: k=6 needs a rented 64-128 core node with at least 1 TB for 3-5 days; the parametric search is CEGIS with algebraic pricing and is open-ended.
Kill: k=6 rank beyond 500k.
P(a) = 0.35. P(b | A, B negative) about 0.30.

**D. Structural program on the span across n=5..10. Rank 4; cheap, parallel, and the only route to all n.**
Mechanism: for n<=10 the systems are tiny. Compute exact left kernels and test whether the dual constraints have a uniform description (the proper-core Lambda functional is one; the G-0181 syzygies hint at deletion-type identities, cluster6). Compute the rank of the vertex-collapse image inside the n=10 loop-inclusive span. Search for n-uniform certificates by imposing structure: collapse-compatibility across all facets, minimal-norm solutions, or support restricted to unicyclic-plus-tree unions, which are the only loop-free full-support non-trees at odd n because 10 edges on 11 vertices with c components have cyclomatic number c-1, and G-0005 kills balanced trees alone (cluster1). *Speculative:* the column fingerprint is the distribution, over vertex orderings, of the signed back-degree word, a shuffle-type invariant of the signed graph; a generating-function description of its linear span would give r(n) in closed form and turn membership into a theorem. I see no further S_11 compression on the row side: the sorted cone already takes the full invariant quotient, and the sketch in A is the correct numerical replacement.
P(a) = 0.90 for the data; P(b) for n=11 about 0.05.

**E. Subdivision constructions a la Bakaev or enlarged first-layer generators as a construction. Rank 5.** Delta_10 needs a subdivision into P^2 cells; the maximum-coordinate cell is not P^2 for d>=5 (G-0067) and the J_2 program was uninformative (G-0082; cluster3). Without residual guidance from A this is a random walk in a continuum. P(a) = 0.30, P(b) = 0.05.

**F. Unconditional refutation. Rank 6; do not fund now.** Requires a valuation on the real polytope algebra separating log[Delta_10] from the span of all log[conv(Z u Z')]; every natural class is excluded by Fact 3. P(candidate in three weeks) = 0.10, P(b) at most 0.02.

First 48 hours: port the DP, validate it column-for-column against the lead's n=9 JSONL, run n=10, and use r(10) to fix m for the n=11 sketch.

## 3. Probabilities

- P(MAX_11 in the loop-free simple-pair k=5 span): 0.55, range 0.45-0.65. For: a perfect record at n=5..10 at k=k_min in this exact family, including odd n=7 and 9 where G-0005 already kills trees alone; the dimension slack at odd n (2k+1 = n) is the same at 11 as at 9; solution spaces are huge, so membership at n<=10 is structural, not accidental. Against: this is precisely the first case nobody could run, and Wang-Basu's failure at k_min for n=8 shows formulations sit near an edge.
- P(MAX_11 in the loop-inclusive k=5 span): 0.62. P(some integer-weight pairwise family): 0.70.
- P(MAX_11 in ReLU_2): 0.72, range 0.6-0.8. Beyond the pairwise ansatz the model has a continuum of generators; the recent history is uniformly "conjectured hierarchies collapse" (log_2 depth, then MAX_5, then n<=10 and all of CPWL_9); no author names an obstacle at 11; and no known invariant could even in principle produce one. A witness at 11 would not by itself lift P(all n) much above 0.6; that needs Path D.

## 4. What the prior campaign got wrong

It optimized for certifiable negatives on convenient subfamilies in a problem where only positives are decisive. Every family was a lift of the MAX10 certificate, presupposing coefficient inheritance that its own G-0114 and G-0115 refuted, and every no-go was then dressed in preregistration, custody, and 23-audit chains (cluster5 estimates 80-85 percent process overhead, with the certified pipeline abandoned at the decisive moment) although none of those negatives could ever bear on MAX_11, which the campaign itself proved in G-0084 and G-0110. Meanwhile the one canonical experiment, the complete Rueß k=5 system, was never assembled, because columns were evaluated in Python at seconds each and the rank was never exploited: the campaign treated a rank-40k problem as a 657k by 12M problem, sampled rows adaptively instead of sketching them, and so refuted one basic solution of a 163k-dimensional affine space every fifteen minutes. The inversion is simple: compile the kernel, run the canonical family to a two-prime decision, lift, and only then let the exact residual choose the enlargement.
