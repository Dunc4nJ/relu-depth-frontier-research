## 1. Leaf induction and its collapse (G-0099 to G-0105, C-0043 to C-0048, D-0005, D-0006)

**The ansatz being lifted.** Rueß et al. write MAX_n as a rational combination of symmetrized atoms Φ_{A,B} = Σ_{σ∈S_n} max(h_{Z_{σA}}, h_{Z_{σB}}), where A and B are multisets of k coordinate edges, Z_A = Σ_{ij∈A}[e_i,e_j] is a graphical zonotope, and k = ⌊(n−1)/2⌋ is forced by their Proposition 4.2 (a simplex is not a signed Minkowski combination of lower-dimensional polytopes; see `literature/papers/2607.21651.txt` lines 444 to 529). So MAX7 needs degree three, MAX10 degree four, MAX11 degree five. A "balanced bicoloured tree" is an atom whose union graph A∪B is a spanning tree with |A| = |B| = (n−1)/2, n odd.

**The proposed operator D.** G-0099 defines, for a tree orbit T on n vertices and a two-component balanced forest orbit F on n−1 vertices, r(T,F) as the number of pairs (leaf edge, opposite-colour edge) whose deletion from T produces F, and sets D(F,T) = n·r(T,F). The factor n is a full-permutation-sum bookkeeping constant, checked entrywise through N_T r(T,F) = n N_F q(F,T) with q the reverse extension count and N the labelled orbit sizes. The hope was that D applied to the tree coefficients of a MAX_n certificate would reproduce the "dominant two-component forest" coefficients of a MAX_{n−1} certificate, so that the public MAX10 certificate would linearly constrain MAX11 tree coefficients.

**G-0099 numbers.** At 6-to-7 there are 53 tree orbits and 11 forest orbits; the complete loop-inclusive degree-three dictionary has 3,010 compressed columns; the joint system is 648 × 3,010 (630 hinge rows, 7 linear rows, 11 incidence rows) with rank 327 and a rational solution of support 113 whose D-image equals the public MAX6 forest coefficients 1/360, −1/1440, −1/360. The public MAX7 certificate has zero balanced-tree terms, so public vectors do not transfer; only a different solution in the 2,683-dimensional nullspace does. A restricted 305 × 110 system (public MAX7 atoms plus trees) has ranks 87/88 mod 1,000,003. At 10-to-11 the 1,387 × 12,459 matrix D has 171,131 nonzeros among 17,280,633 entries and full row rank 1,387 modulo 1,000,003, hence over Q, so the 252-term dominant-c2 projection of the public MAX10 certificate has a rational tree preimage. G-0099 itself flags this as "vacuous as a constraint at the forest-coefficient level" because every target has a preimage. **Why "coefficient gauge, not semantic".** The exact face operator is L_u f(x) = lim_{t→∞}(f(tu+x) − t f(u)) for a fixed covector u; it is linear in f and equals h_{face_u(P)} on support functions (G-0105 verifies this as a positive control, preserving the mixed relation and rejecting a mutation with residual 4428). D instead chooses u, the events, and their number from the atom's graph presentation, is defined only on tree columns, and assigns different lower images to two coefficient vectors representing the same function. It is therefore a linear map on coefficient vectors, not on functions or polytopes. G-0105 (`corrected_face_operator.py`, verdict FAIL) confirmed this on the complete n=5 quotient with the corrected strict-ridge map as well (rank 17 rises to 22, eight-term witness evaluating to −2880 at x=(−3,−3,−3,−3)), showed the corrected map already fails on trees via F_0 − 2F_1 + F_3 = 0 evaluating to 60 at x=(−3,−3,−3,−1) (and the 1,−3,3,−1 relation on n=7 orbits 0,1,4,11), and proved that the natural strict-ridge extension has no non-tree terms at all, since n−3 surviving occurrences in three components force a forest. G-0105 lists what a valid induction would still need: a representation-independent operator on the complete degree-five quotient, a derived exact action on MAX11 and MAX10 including tagged and linear pieces, and a reverse lifting theorem. Its first run spent 31 minutes in a SymPy fraction-free rank and was interrupted; the replacement checks kernel annihilation.
