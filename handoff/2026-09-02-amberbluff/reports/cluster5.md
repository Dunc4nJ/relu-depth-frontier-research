# Audit of G-0116 to G-0178: the exact global-residual CEGIS loop on the 163,740-column MAX10-lift family

**Bottom line.** Twelve finite-row members of the frozen 163,740-column family were solved exactly over Q, and every one was refuted by a complete ordered-chamber normal form. Nonzero-residual counts never shrank across the sequence. The certified custody pipeline was abandoned at the end, and the last three iterations ran uncertified in /tmp. The ledger footprint of the whole range is one experiment entry, EXP-0026, and zero claims or evidence entries. The one durable structural fact is a certified exact rank: the 540-row family matrix has rank 349 over Q, and 349 columns span all 163,740.

## 1. The loop and its vocabulary

**Dictionary.** The columns are not the k=5 ansatz. G-0113c/d froze a source-derived subfamily: each of the 402 pair templates of the public degree-four MAX10 certificate is embedded on 11 labels, one nonloop edge is appended to each branch, and the two edges must be distinct and either endpoint-disjoint or share exactly one endpoint. The 1,193,940 raw extensions collapse under common-edge cancellation, S_11 relabelling and branch swap to 163,740 signed-W classes, all loopless. The audit at `artifacts/reviews/G-0130-model-boundary/AUDIT_VERDICT.md` states the inclusion chain U_163740 within V_11,5 within N^(2)_11 and the comparison censuses: 754,017 loopless degree-five classes, 7,015,841 loop-inclusive records plus the 5E and 5L carriers, and 12,179,657 raw Rueß templates. A separator for this family would therefore say nothing about the degree-five ansatz, and the authors say so in every claim-boundary string.

**Atom and target.** For record s, F_s is the full labelled S_11 orbit sum of max(S_A, S_B), where S_A is the sum of pairwise maxima over the edge multiset A. The candidate identity is sum_s a_s F_s = L * 11! * max(x) with integer a_s and positive integer scale L.

**Complete normal form.** On the open chamber x_0 < ... < x_10, every F_s equals a linear form plus a sum of h_d ReLU(d.x) over primitive zero-sum integer directions d with first nonzero coordinate positive, retained iff some proper prefix sum is negative, which is exactly when the hyperplane meets the chamber interior. The kernel enumerates all 11! labelled permutations per term via active-vertex injections times an inactive-label factorial, reading one raw back-degree word in [-5,5]^11 per permutation. The universe of such active directions for degree-five blocks is 657,822 per G-0109. The lemma in `artifacts/math/G-0117/NORMAL_FORM_UNIQUENESS_LEMMA.md` proves the retained hinge functions are linearly independent modulo linear functions on the chamber, by a gradient-jump argument at a generic point of each hyperplane. An all-zero aggregate is therefore a global identity on R^11 by permutation symmetry and continuity, and any nonzero hinge coefficient is an exact refutation. The lemma is elementary and correct. One overflow bug was found and fixed by review, recorded in `artifacts/math/G-0117/OVERFLOW_FIX.md`: scale times coordinate was computed in i8 and wrapped for a valid direction with coordinates outside [-5,5].

**Panel.** The 301 panel rows are the G-0111/G-0081 formal-colour assignment-sum functionals, which are evaluation rows that do not need the 11! enumeration. G-0116 evaluates them per record with a cycle-cut tree DP, enumerating only 4^|F| colours on a feedback vertex set, with median 22x speedup on cyclic active-10/11 controls; see `artifacts/math/G-0116/cycle_cut_panel_benchmark_v1.json`. G-0117 used it to build the 301 by 163,740 signed-i128 cache of 788,571,840 bytes in 4,226 s. Modular scans put the panel rank on the family near 115, so 301 rows carry only about 115 independent constraints.

**Coordinate pricing.** For a direction d, the price row is h_d(W) for all 163,740 records, computed by the G-0117 Stage-C subset DP over signed scales without enumerating 11!. One direction takes about 16 s in G-0170, 32 took 135 s in G-0127, and 128 took 515 s in the G-0176 receipt.

**Loop.** Solve the accumulated rows over Q by exact column generation: FLINT rank, derive an integer left separator, scan all 163,740 columns in sequence order for the first nonzero price, append, repeat until the target enters the span. The fresh-Q invariant reopens all columns every iteration rather than freezing the prior support. Clear denominators, then run the complete BigInt global replay and subtract L * 11! from linear coordinate 10. On a nonzero residual, take the signed-lexicographically first K nonzero directions, price them, and append them as zero-target rows. Batch32 is K=32 in G-0118 iteration 4, G-0126 and G-0135. Pool128 is P=128 with exact rank-aware admission of the first 32 rank-growing rows in G-0140. Fresh128 is the 128-direction residual prefix of the G-0164 member, priced by the G-0172 pricer. The first-row or fresh-direction rank gate in G-0170/G-0168 asks whether the single first fresh direction row h adds rank to the rank-349 540-row matrix, via the Schur complement S^T lambda = h[B] and a scan of Delta_j = h_j minus lambda^T A[R,j]. A dependent and target-incompatible row would be a family separator.

**Trend.** Not shrinking. Nonzero directions oscillate between 147k and 196k while the row count triples, and more than 99.7 percent of supported directions are nonzero in every replay. The first nonzero direction crawls leftward in lex order, which lex-first selection guarantees regardless of any progress toward an identity. Row-system rank grew 349, 468, 595, 659, adding about 120 of every 128 fresh rows until the duplicate-pair batch added only 64. With column rank at most 163,740, this schedule needs on the order of 1,300 more batches, each roughly 6 min of global replay plus 9 min of pricing plus a solve. The structural cause is stated in the G-0164 preregistration itself: a nonzero residual refutes only one member of a large affine finite-row solution space. At 540 rows the rank is 349 in 163,740 unknowns, so each iteration tests one basic solution of an affine space of dimension about 163,391. This is a treadmill, not a converging loop.

**What the rank-aware Stage-C selector did.** `artifacts/math/G-0140/stage_c_selector/complete_matrix_rank_selector_v1.py` used FFPACK modular pivot proposals, exact FLINT RREF, and a left-annihilator scan of all 163,740 columns. It also exposed that the earlier masters' reported "rank" was the rank of the selected columns when column generation stopped, not the full-matrix rank: G-0135's 204 versus the true 221. Its by-product was that all 128 rows could be admitted at once. Stages D and E were never run; the manifest's planned outputs `rank_aware_master_result_v1.json` and `new_member_global_replay_v1.json` do not exist.

## 5. The stop decision

G-0178 asked whether the first 4,096 nonzero residual directions hide a sparse linear obstruction worth pricing over the family. Two disjoint random 512-record panels, quotiented by the rank-659 basis at primes 1,000,003 and 1,000,033, gave quotient rank 512 and augmented 513 per panel and 1,024 versus 1,025 combined, which the README itself calls dimension-driven. Among 440 predeclared supports per prime, no circuit annihilated both panels and paired nonzero with the residual; 158 of 3,584 panel-1 circuits survived panel 2, all with zero residual pairing. Classification: DEPENDENCY_RICH_BUT_RESIDUAL_COMPATIBLE_ON_PROBES. Enforced action in `artifacts/math/G-0178-rank659-fingerprint-screen/README.md`: do not full-price another residual batch from this path; advance to the loop-bearing STAR-outside-primary slice. This is T0 exploratory evidence with no source or result audit. The pivot became G-0179: the 5,773 common-apex STAR classes with one residual loop carry hinges with d_0 = 1 that MAX11 lacks, so a nonsingular 5,771 by 5,771 restriction would prove STAR adds only a direct summand irrelevant to MAX11. That gate failed at rank 5,291 at both primes with 90 duplicate column pairs, and the campaign moved to classifying the 478-dimensional restriction kernel modulo the old span, which is where ledger claims C-0049 and C-0052 finally appear.

## 7. Dead routes, next thoughts, fragilities

Died: lex-first residual CEGIS on this family; the algebraic and rooted recurrence transport laws of G-0115, G-0119, G-0120 and G-0124; the certified G-0140 D/E and G-0168 pipelines. The authors' next thought was the STAR loop quarantine and kernel classification modulo the old span, and there was no return to the 163,740 loop.

Soundness: the refutations are sound. Certificates are denominator-cleared integers, a nonzero residue modulo either prime of an integer is nonzero, and from G-0126 onward the aggregates are unconditional BigInt anyway. Memberships are exact-Q with all-row replay and +1 coefficient mutants. G-0140's rank 349 is exact over Q. G-0176's 659 is a modular-witnessed nonsingular minor plus an exact solve, sound as a rank lower bound. The pair-block rank 64 is modular only, so it is a lower bound on the rational rank of that block, not an upper bound.

Fragile or overstated: the phrase "all 163,740 records" invites misreading as the degree-five ansatz, while it is 2.2 percent of the loopless denominator and 1.3 percent of the raw template count. Each member is an arbitrary basic solution of a rank-at-most-659 system in 163,740 unknowns, so refuting it is nearly information-free about the family. The G-0135 "rank 204" was a selected-column rank, not the full-matrix rank 221. Lex-first directions and the panel are structured selections, not samples; the only random sampling is G-0178's two panels, explicitly bounded. All review is same-lineage T1 by GPT-5 Codex agents with no T2 transport. The 789 MB cache and 160 MB matrices are hash-pinned but outside Git, and the final negatives live in /tmp.

Alternative not taken: the loop is a lazy row-generation approximation to one linear-algebra question, whether the target lies in the column space of the full 657,833 by 163,740 normal-form matrix. Complete normal forms cost about 1.2 s per term on 12 threads, so all 163,740 columns are roughly two to three CPU-days, and a two-prime rank and consistency test of the resulting sparse matrix would decide the family question directly instead of testing one basic solution every fifteen minutes.
