# Audit of artifacts G-0063 through G-0084

Scope: I read every README, RESULT, THEOREM and audit document in `artifacts/math/G-0063` through `G-0084`, the cleanroom audits for G-0073, G-0074, G-0078, G-0079, G-0081, G-0083 and G-0084, claims C-0025 to C-0042, dead-ends D-0003 and D-0004, gaps G-0009 to G-0014, reviews V-0003 to V-0006, the G-0081 and G-0070 result JSON headers, and the Bakaev–Yehudayoff source PDF in `artifacts/math/G-0063/source`. I also read G-0022, which is the equivalence theorem the whole range rests on, and G-0085 and G-0086, which are the artifacts behind claims C-0039 and C-0040 even though their numbers fall outside the range. The "G-0012 gluing gap" is ledger gap G-0012 in `ledger/gaps.toml`, not an artifact.

**Bottom line.** Nothing in this range decides MAX11. Three results are genuinely unrestricted necessary conditions over R: G-0063's stabilizer size bound, G-0064's tied-face theorem, and G-0083's hinge-rank bound. All three are weak. The only exact nonmembership theorem over R concerns one ad hoc finite family (G-0078), and G-0081 extends it at a single prime. Every wider obstruction is conditional on an unproved normal-form bridge that G-0084 names and partially falsifies. The constructive gluing route is stuck at off-facet wall cancellation, gap G-0012, not at gluing.

## Framework and structural theorems

**1. The reformulation.** Work in H, the zero-sum hyperplane of R^11, with mu the all-ones vector over 11 and the centered simplex Delta^0 = conv{e_i - mu}. Its support function is max(x) minus the mean. A zonotope in H is a translate of a finite Minkowski sum of segments. The class P^2, written S2(H) in G-0022 and P_{n,2} in Bakaev–Yehudayoff, is the set of finite Minkowski sums of primitive blocks conv(P cup Q) with P, Q zonotopes. G-0022 Theorem 1: MAX_n has an exact two-hidden-layer ReLU representation with arbitrary real weights, arbitrary width and no skips if and only if Delta_n^0 + A = B for some A, B in P^2, and A, B may be taken S_n-invariant. The proof is the per-neuron identity ReLU(h_P - h_Q) = h_{conv(P cup Q)} - h_Q, where P and Q are the zonotopes built from the positive and negative first-layer coefficients feeding one second neuron. A "virtual polytope" is the formal difference [B] - [A]; W_2 in G-0082 is the class of polytopes X with X + A = B for A, B in P^2; the "stabilizer" is A. G-0022 Theorem 5 shows Delta_n^0 lies in P^2 only for n at most 4, so any MAX11 certificate needs a genuinely nonpoint A, which the campaign calls virtual cancellation.

Glossary for the remaining terms. The exposed face F_u(K) is the set of maximizers of u on K; at d_i = mu - e_i the exposed face of Delta^0 is the centered MAX10 simplex opposite vertex i, and a global zonotope generator g survives on that face exactly when g_i = 0 (G-0064 eq. 19), all other generators contributing a translation only. Support height means h_K(d_i); G-0085 computes 1/11 for Delta^0. Generalized permutohedra are polytopes whose edges are parallel to roots e_a - e_b. RANGE11 is max minus min (G-0085); its centered polytope is the difference body Delta^0 + (-Delta^0). "Eleven-face zonotope gluing" is G-0086: find global zonotopes P, Q whose eleven exposed faces at the d_i are prescribed local branch pairs up to translation. J_p is the join of a p-simplex with a square, of dimension p + 3; J_2 in R^5 is the cone over the G-0031 cell and G-0035 built a wall library for subdividing it; J_7 has dimension 10 and G-0082 shows its membership in W_2 is equivalent to MAX11 membership. A Y-spoke is Y_{k,l,11} = max(2x_k, x_l + x_11), whose wall x_l + x_11 - 2x_k = 0 has support three and is not a braid hyperplane; a Y-spoke atom is max(A + 2x_k, B + Y) or the reversed orientation, where (A, B) is one of the 252 pinned two-component MAX10 base terms, so both branches have degree six. The carriers are C_L = Sym_avg(x_1), C_E = Sym_avg(max(x_1, x_2)), C_Y = Sym_avg(max(2x_1, x_2 + x_3)); in G-0072 the carriers 5E and 5L are the degree-five sums of five common non-loop or five loop generators. A profile over levels {0,1,2,3} is a count vector of how many of the eleven coordinates sit at each level; its row is the sum over all distinct labelled assignments, which equals the assignment count times the symmetrized atom at one representative point. There are 364 four-level profiles, 78 three-level profiles and 120 profiles using all four levels. Farey F6 enters because, on three-valued inputs normalized to (0, t, 1), every branch switch of a Y-spoke atom is affine in t with coefficients bounded by six, so all roots lie among the 13 fractions with denominator at most six and everything is affine between them. "Same-component closure" is G-0079, which adds the leaves l lying in the same MAX10 forest component as k. "Schur quotient" is G-0081: eliminate the rank-6,876 old basis and row-reduce the target-last complement modulo p; the full rank is 6,876 plus the Schur rank.

**2. The structural theorems, with exact hypotheses.**

| artifact | object constrained | over R | about unrestricted networks |
|---|---|---|---|
| G-0063 | Delta + A = B with A, B in P^2 | yes | yes via G-0022, but only a size bound on A |
| G-0065, G-0066 | A a single zonotope | yes | no, a subclass no known certificate inhabits |
| G-0067 | one specific P^2 subdivision cell | yes | no, subdivisions are a sufficient route only |
| G-0083 hinge-rank | any two-hidden-layer rep of a non-zero-summand target | yes | yes, but nearly vacuous at d = 10 |
| G-0083 eight-primitive no-go | dictionaries from the eight T_{3,2} primitives | yes | no |
| G-0084 Theorem 2 | symmetrization of an arbitrary rep | yes | yes, with negative content |
| G-0084 Theorem 5 | pair-max orbit spaces | yes | a counterexample to a bridge, not about MAX11 |

G-0063 imports Bakaev–Yehudayoff Theorem 7, which I checked in the source: rho_Delta(P) is at most 2^d - 1 whenever the depth d is below ceil(log2(n + 1)); for H of dimension 10 and d = 2 that hypothesis holds. With outer additivity of simplices and the reflection identity rho(-A) = 1/rho(A), the identity Delta_10 + A = B forces 1/3 <= rho(A) < 3 and lambda_Delta(A) >= 21/8, or 7/2 when A is centrally symmetric. The six public MAX5 to MAX10 certificates satisfy the identities with large margins, so the bound is validated but not sharp. It is a necessary size condition and excludes nothing anyone proposed.

G-0065 applies that bound facewise to Delta_10 + Z = B with Z one zonotope. Each s-way tie face gives lambda_{Delta_{s-1}}(F(Z)) >= (s - 4)/2; double counting over 7-sets gives lambda_Delta(Z) >= 55/14 with equality only for one fourteenth of the K_11 graphical zonotope, and the naive centered Delta-zonotope is excluded outright because its facet faces are points, which would put Delta_9 in P^2. G-0066 classifies the extremal case inside generalized permutohedra: a nonpoint generalized permutohedron in P^2 has rho = 3 exactly when it is a translated nonnegative sum of coordinate tetrahedra. The G-0065 equality case then fails, because its 7- and 8-way tie faces are the polytopes D_7 and D_8, whose unique signed-simplex coefficients are not tetrahedral, so the bound becomes strict. A hostile control in R^5 shows the braid-fan hypothesis is essential. Both are theorems over R about a subclass, and the G-0065 verifier itself records that every public certificate has rho(A) different from 1, so no known solution has a zonotope stabilizer.

G-0067 computes the complete Minkowski type cone of the maximum-coordinate subdivision cell D_d and shows no nonzero point of it is a primitive P^2 block for d >= 5, because a centrally symmetric branch can hold at most two of the d axis vertices. Z3 confirms d = 4 SAT and d = 5, 6, 7 UNSAT in `verification.json`. This kills one input to Bakaev et al. Lemma 10 and nothing else.

G-0083 has no review. Its hinge-rank statement is the one unrestricted item: if h_P has a two-hidden-layer representation and P is a full-dimensional non-zero-summand d-polytope, then after taking the recession function and separating affine terms, the span of the first-layer hinge directions has dimension at least d - 1. The proof is that every second-layer unit is h_{conv(t + Z_+, Z_-)} - h_{Z_-} with both zonotopes parallel to that span, so a smaller span makes P a signed combination of zero-volume polytopes. For MAX11 this says rank at least 9 inside a 10-dimensional space. The literature audit in `artifacts/cleanroom/G-0083/hinge-rank-literature-audit.md` found no verbatim prior but classifies it as an unstated narrow synthesis of Hertrich et al. equation 13, Grillo–Hofmann Lemmas 9 and 10 and Koutschan et al. Corollary 5.2, and warns against any "first rank lower bound" wording. The eight-primitive no-go is exact: the six pair maxima and a, b share a six-dimensional direction span, so every atom has dimension at most 7 while the T_{3,2} Newton polytope has dimension 10 and is not a zero-summand. The note also corrects a proposed ten-chamber reduction and gives a crude order-type ceiling of 525,525.

G-0084 Theorem 2 is exact and unrestricted: symmetrizing any bias-free two-hidden-layer representation of a symmetric positively homogeneous function yields an orbit average of virtual atoms [conv(P_j cup Q_j)] - [Q_j] with arbitrary real generator directions, and nothing more. Theorem 5 gives the explicit function G, the average over 990 ordered triples of max(6x_i, 5x_j + x_l) divided by 5940, which is symmetric, convex, homogeneous, translation covariant, in the degree-six pair-max orbit space, and realizable by a two-hidden-layer network, yet outside the degree-five orbit space plus affine functions because its sorted-cone hinge normal 6e_2 - 5e_1 - e_3 has signed mass six. The missing bridges are named precisely: GNF, arbitrary network to graphical pair atoms, and DR5-MAX, the hinge-free-fibre degree reduction. The hostile referee found both theorems consistent and generalized Theorem 5 to a strict filtration in every n and k.

## The gluing route and the Y-spoke program

**3. Where the constructive route is stuck.** G-0064 is an unrestricted theorem over R. Lemma 2 shows a summand of centrally symmetric sets is centrally symmetric. Theorem 3 and Corollary 4 then show that at any input where three or more coordinates tie for the maximum, the exposed face of Delta^0 is a simplex of dimension at least two, so some output-active second neuron has zero preactivation there and a tied, non-centrally-symmetric face. Corollary 5 uses Baire to show that for each of the 1,980 proper faces some second neuron vanishes on a relatively open patch of the tie cone. Corollary 6 states that every MAX11 certificate restricts to a MAX10 certificate on each facet cone and that the eleven restrictions must be faces of common global zonotopes. The note also proves that MAX11 is not a signed sum of dense compositions MAX10(L_r x) plus a linear term, by Rueß Proposition 4.2. Only finite controls are scripted in `exact_face_gluing_checks.py`; the theorem is prose.

G-0086, claim C-0040, then solves the eleven-face problem: given local branch pairs with generators in E_i, tied relative centers, and generator consistency across E_i cap E_j, global full-dimensional P and Q exist with the prescribed faces up to one translation per facet, and consistency is also necessary. The face centers are steered by 110 dense correctors that form a basis of H^11. Reviewed T1 holds as V-0006. G-0085, claim C-0039, shows the sufficiency step fails: RANGE11 has centered polytope Delta^0 + (-Delta^0), its eleven projected facet faces are exactly the centered MAX10 simplex, yet its support heights are 1 rather than 1/11 and it has 2046 facets rather than eleven. Since RANGE11 is itself a global two-hidden-layer network, conditional on the cited MAX1 to MAX10 networks in C-0003, even demanding one common global network does not help. That is dead-end D-0004.

So the route is not stuck at gluing. It is stuck exactly where gap G-0012 says: every dense corrector and padding segment adds a global wall away from the facets, and no theorem or construction cancels those walls throughout the unique-maximum cones while preserving the 1/11 support heights. G-0022 Theorem 6 gives the local law any cancellation must obey, namely that every non-braid effective kink inside a first-layer chamber cancels projectively among at least two second neurons. No family with that property has been produced.

G-0080 closes the last of 258 seeded arrangements in the G-0035 wall library for J_2: all eight full-dimensional cells of the displayed three-wall arrangement have two-ray type cones, and a two-center necessary condition is UNSAT on the obstructed ray and the entire positive projective interior, with 22 Z3 proofs. G-0082 then observes that J_2 is already in W_2 by the published dimension-nine theorem, so every J_2 subdivision failure is a method falsifier and says nothing about MAX11. G-0082's own content is the equivalence J_7 in W_2 iff MAX11 in W_2 via Bakaev et al. Claim 5, the reduction to T_{3,2} through at most 81 affine copies, the fact that J_p is never a zero-summand, and the exact dead end of treating the three squares as black-box scalars. It says it received an internal adversarial proof review, but I found no review file or ledger entry for G-0082.

G-0081, claim C-0041, keeps all 26,689 columns without price filtering. **7. Overstated, circular, or fragile.**

- Every finite-family separation is conditional on GNF. G-0084 Theorem 2 shows symmetrization yields atoms with arbitrary real generators, and Theorem 5 shows the degree-five pair-max restriction does not follow from symmetry. G-0078 and G-0081 therefore bound a family chosen because it "escapes the root-only obstruction," nothing more. The READMEs say so, but the custody apparatus around G-0081 makes the result look weightier than its claim.
- The Y-spoke family is outside even the Rueß pair-max ansatz, since its walls are not braid hyperplanes, so its failure does not bear on gap G-0014.
- G-0063, G-0065 and G-0066 constrain a subclass no known certificate inhabits, and the one unrestricted bound is far from tight.
- G-0064's "precise remaining lift problem" turned out to be always solvable in G-0086 and insufficient in G-0085; the real gap is wall cancellation, so the G-0064 framing is superseded.
- The J_2 program in G-0031, G-0035 and G-0080 tested a sufficient method on a polytope already known to be in W_2; G-0082 concedes those negatives are uninformative for MAX11.
- G-0081 is one prime, same code, same host, with no review of the executed runner and no ledger review for C-0041.
- G-0084's "logically weakest" DR5-MAX is trivial as the referee notes, and the requested presentation fixes were not applied.
- G-0083 is unreviewed, its literature audit warns against novelty wording, and its hinge-rank bound is nearly vacuous at d = 10.
- The G-0073 and G-0074 witnesses are basic solutions with a 1,614-bit common denominator and 7,849 free directions; "survivor" means only that a necessary finite test passed.
- G-0075 first claimed 62 and 122 occupied cells; `ARRANGEMENT_AUDIT.md` corrects this to 56 and 108 open cells and notes a complete finite membership test would need 189,000 rows, so the 16,738-row system suffices for nonmembership but never for membership.
- C-0039 depends on the cited, never replayed, MAX1 to MAX10 networks in C-0003.
- The sketch gates in G-0068 and G-0072 are correctly one-sided, and G-0068's 4,390 deficiency is properly not promoted.

I found no circularity in the exact theorems themselves. The one near-circular item is DR5-MAX, which restates the conclusion once GNF is assumed.
