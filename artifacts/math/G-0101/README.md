# G-0101 — Exact full-dimensional filter for active signed-mass-five atoms

## Result

Let

\[
Z_A=\sum_{e\in A}[e_u,e_v],\qquad
Z_B=\sum_{e\in B}[e_u,e_v],\qquad
R_{A,B}=\operatorname{conv}(Z_A\cup Z_B),
\]

where both branches contain five edge occurrences, `A` and `B` are disjoint
after cancellation (signed mass five), and the union is active on all `n=11`
coordinates. Loops `[e_i,e_i]` are allowed. Let `G` be the residual union
multigraph, let `c` be the
number of coordinate components of its nonloop support, and define

\[
\delta_C=|B\cap E(C)|-|A\cap E(C)|
\]

for each component `C`, counting loops as one edge occurrence.

Then

\[
\boxed{\dim R_{A,B}=11-c+\mathbf 1_{\delta\ne0}.}
\]

Since both branches have equal degree, `sum_C delta_C=0`. Therefore the atom
is ten-dimensional exactly when

1. `c=1`; or
2. `c=2` and the two branches have unequal edge-occurrence mass on either
   component.

Applied to the hash-bound G-0038 active signed-mass-five universe:

| stratum | orbit count | dimension status |
|---|---:|---|
| connected union graph | 12,459 | full |
| two components, nonzero imbalance | 83,595 | full |
| two components, zero imbalance | 10,232 | deficient |
| three or more components | 278,139 | deficient |
| **total** | **384,425** | **96,054 full; 288,371 deficient** |

Thus, within the active signed-mass-five stratum, the complete set of
individually full-dimensional atoms has **96,054** symmetry orbits, not the
provisional 106,286 obtained by keeping every two-component atom.

## Proof

Orient each nonloop edge arbitrarily. The direction space contributed by the
two zonotopes together is

\[
W=\operatorname{span}\{e_v-e_u:uv\in E(G)\}.
\]

For a graph on all eleven active coordinates with `c` components,
`dim W=11-c`. The affine hull of `R_{A,B}` is generated by `W` and one branch
offset `z_B-z_A`, so its dimension can exceed `dim W` by at most one.

The quotient `R^11/W` records exactly the total coordinate mass on each
component. Every endpoint choice of a branch edge contributes one unit to
the component containing that edge. Consequently the image of `z_B-z_A` in
the quotient is precisely `delta`. It survives modulo `W` iff `delta` is
nonzero, proving the displayed formula. Loops affect `delta` but add no edge
direction, exactly as required.

## Epistemic boundary

This theorem determines which individual active signed-mass-five atoms can
carry ten-dimensional volume. It does **not** prove that rank-deficient atoms,
or the lower-signed-mass degree-five strata with common padding, can be
discarded from a semantic representation: they may be necessary correction
terms. A staged search may first ask whether MAX11 lies in the span of the
96,054 full atoms, but a negative answer is not a complete degree-five no-go
unless a separate redundancy theorem eliminates every omitted correction.

Likewise, the frozen degree-five graphical dictionary is a Rueß/pair-max
ansatz, not the class of all unrestricted two-hidden-layer ReLU networks.
