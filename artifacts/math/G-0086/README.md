# G-0086 — exact exposed-face gluing for zonotopal branches

## Theorem (n=11)

Let

\[
H=\{z\in\mathbb R^{11}:\sum_k z_k=0\},\qquad
d_i=\mu-e_i,\qquad
E_i=H\cap\{z_i=0\}=H\cap d_i^\perp,
\]

and write the centered segment `S(u)=[-u/2,u/2]`.

For every `i`, let `P_i,Q_i subset H` be translated zonotopes whose generator
directions lie in `E_i`.  Write their centers as `p_i,q_i` and assume the
branch-tie condition

\[
p_i-q_i\in E_i.                                           \tag{A}
\]

Consolidate parallel centered segments and orient each unoriented generator
line by one fixed canonical sign convention.  Assume separately for the
`P`-branch and the `Q`-branch:

> **Generator consistency (GC).** If a generator line lies in
> `E_i intersect E_j`, its total segment length is identical in the local data
> at `i` and `j`, with absence interpreted as length zero.

Then finite full-dimensional zonotopes `P,Q subset H` and translations
`t_i in H` exist such that, simultaneously for all eleven normals,

\[
F_{d_i}(P)=P_i+t_i,
\qquad
F_{d_i}(Q)=Q_i+t_i.                                      \tag{1}
\]

Consequently, for `C_i=conv(P_i union Q_i)`,

\[
F_{d_i}(\operatorname{conv}(P\cup Q))=C_i+t_i.            \tag{2}
\]

Conversely, GC is necessary for an ordered global branch pair satisfying
(1).  Thus, conditional on (A), GC is necessary and sufficient for gluing the
eleven specified exposed faces modulo common translations.  The word
"facet" is justified only when the corresponding local face spans `E_i`.

## Step 1 — exact face formula and visible generators

For

\[
Z=c+\sum_r S(u_r),
\]

the identity `d_i dot u=-u_i` gives

\[
F_{d_i}(Z)=c+
\sum_{u_{r,i}\ne0}\left\{-\frac{\operatorname{sgn}(u_{r,i})}{2}u_r\right\}
+\sum_{u_{r,i}=0}S(u_r).                                  \tag{3}
\]

A generator survives as a segment on face `i` exactly when `u_i=0`.
Canonical zonotope generator lines and their consolidated lengths are
intrinsic edge data.  Therefore a global generator visible on faces `i` and
`j` has the same segment on both, proving necessity of GC.  Conversely, GC
allows every visible local generator line to be included exactly once in a
global branch, reproducing every centered local face shape.

## Step 2 — dense generators steer every relative face center

Dense generators have no zero coordinate, so (3) exposes only a point at
each of the eleven normals and changes no local face shape.

For `a in [11]` define

\[
b^a=10e_a-\sum_{j\ne a}e_j,
\qquad
u^{a,r}=b^a+\tfrac12(e_r-e_a),\quad r\ne a.               \tag{4}
\]

The coordinates of `u^{a,r}` are `19/2` at `a`, `-1/2` at `r`, and `-1`
elsewhere.  Hence every vector is dense and lies in `H`.  For fixed `a`, the
ten vectors `u^{a,r}` form a basis of `H`.

Put

\[
\tau_i(u)=-\frac{\operatorname{sgn}(u_i)}2u,
\qquad
\Theta(u)=(\tau_1(u),\ldots,\tau_{11}(u))\in H^{11}.
\]

Let `s^a in R^11` have entry `-1` at `a` and `+1` elsewhere.  Then

\[
\Theta(u^{a,r})=\tfrac12s^a\otimes u^{a,r}.               \tag{5}
\]

The eleven vectors `s^a` form a basis of `R^11`: their matrix is `J-2I`,
with eigenvalues `9,-2,...,-2`.  Together with the ten bases of `H`, equation
(5) proves that the 110 vectors `Theta(u^{a,r})` form a basis of

\[
\mathbb R^{11}\otimes H\cong H^{11}.                     \tag{6}
\]

Let `epsilon_i in H` be the desired relative branch-center correction after
the visible generators are glued.  With `E=sum_i epsilon_i`, set

\[
v_a=-\epsilon_a+\frac19E,
\qquad
\alpha_{a,r}=2(v_a)_r+\frac4{19}(v_a)_a.                  \tag{7}
\]

Direct substitution gives

\[
\sum_{r\ne a}\alpha_{a,r}u^{a,r}=v_a,
\qquad
\frac12\sum_a s^a\otimes v_a=(\epsilon_i)_i.             \tag{8}
\]

For positive `alpha`, add `S(alpha*u)` to `P`; for negative `alpha`, add
`S((-alpha)*u)` to `Q`.  The latter changes the `P-Q` face-center difference
by `alpha*Theta(u)`, so (8) realizes every correction exactly.

## Step 3 — full dimension, common translations, and compilation

For one fixed `a_0`, the zonotope generated by the ten dense basis vectors
`u^{a_0,r}` is full-dimensional in `H` and exposes a point at every `d_i`.
Adding the same positive multiple of it to `P` and `Q` makes both branches
full-dimensional without changing their relative face data.

After the correction, define `t_i` by matching the `P`-face center.  Equation
(A) makes the same `t_i` match the `Q`-face center and ties their support
heights.  Exposed-face calculus for a tied convex hull gives (2).

The primitive support function compiles without skip connections:

\[
h_{\operatorname{conv}(P\cup Q)}
=\rho(h_P-h_Q)+h_Q.                                       \tag{9}
\]

The first hidden layer computes the signed segment support functions via

\[
h_{S(u)}(x)=\tfrac12\rho(u^Tx)+\tfrac12\rho(-u^Tx).
\]

The second hidden layer computes `rho(h_P-h_Q)`.  If translated centers make
`h_Q` signed, propagate it through two second-layer units as
`rho(h_Q)-rho(-h_Q)`.  Linear center terms are represented by paired
first-layer ReLUs.  Thus each constructed primitive block is an exact finite
two-hidden-layer ReLU function.

## What this closes

The theorem closes the following local-to-global subproblem from G-0064:

> once ordered local branch generators satisfy GC and the branch supports
> tie, can their eleven exposed faces be realized by one full-dimensional
> pair of global zonotopes?

The answer is yes, constructively and over the rationals whenever the local
data are rational.  Completely asymmetric local generator lines having `i`
as their only zero coordinate impose no cross-facet GC constraint.

## What this does not close

The theorem controls only the eleven selected exposed faces and their local
support germs.  Every dense correction or padding segment adds a global wall
`u^perp` away from those faces.  It does not prove that these walls cancel in
a signed collection of primitive blocks, that the resulting global virtual
polytope is a simplex, or that MAX11 is representable.

G-0085 shows why this distinction is decisive: a genuine global two-hidden-
layer RANGE11 network already reproduces all eleven projected MAX10 face
shapes while possessing many additional walls.  Any target-facing use of the
present theorem must preserve exact facet support heights and prove
cancellation on every unique-maximum cone.

## Epistemic boundary and falsifiers

The construction received a fresh-context same-family T1 hostile audit.  The
reviewer independently checked (3), intrinsic generator consistency, the
110-vector basis, formulas (7)--(8), signed branch allocation, full-dimensional
padding, common translation, branch tie, and no-skip compilation.  No novelty
search has been completed, no T2/human referee is available, and no priority
claim is made.

The theorem is falsified by local data satisfying GC and (A) for which the
construction fails to give (1), a hidden compatibility not encoded by
canonical generator lines, a linear dependence in (6), an incorrect signed
allocation in (8), or a compilation that requires a skip connection or third
hidden layer.  The audit found none under the repaired hypotheses.
