# Exact-neural-representation epistemics

W11 translation for the mathematical subfield of exact ReLU representation. Translator: `campaign-bootstrap/CrimsonBirch` · 2026-08-29 UTC.

## 1. Exact object

A claim is draft unless it pins: input domain; target function; quantifier order; activation convention; affine/bias convention; hidden-layer counting; finite widths; coefficient field; equality regime; allowed connections/gates; success artifact; and falsifier. “Depth two” without this tuple is not a statement.

## 2. Evidence ladder

`ASSERTED -> CITED -> COMPUTED_BOUNDED -> INDEPENDENTLY_REPLAYED -> REFEREED -> FORMALIZED`

| Transition | Required obligations |
|---|---|
| ASSERTED to CITED | primary source retrieved; SHA-256/source card; exact locator and short excerpt; statement-match and cousin check |
| CITED to COMPUTED_BOUNDED | preregistered exact domain; implementation identity; deterministic artifact; detection floor; all six controls green |
| COMPUTED_BOUNDED to INDEPENDENTLY_REPLAYED | clean implementation/author path; independently regenerated output; exact identity comparison |
| INDEPENDENTLY_REPLAYED to REFEREED | T2+ capable-of-failure review; theorem/certificate reconstructed; no open load-bearing gaps |
| REFEREED to FORMALIZED | Lean/kernel artifact; import/axiom trust scan; informal-formal statement match; pinned toolchain |

Theorems do not decay merely with time, but frontier/novelty status does. Any new source or changed target convention makes statement-match/novelty evidence stale until rerun.

## 3. Failure taxonomy

| Core mode | Local form | Tell |
|---|---|---|
| Lookahead | designing the certificate class after inspecting MAX11 solver failures | restrictions or thresholds appear only after results |
| Survivorship | reporting the one ansatz/seed/ordering that solved | failed/aborted trials absent from `experiments.toml` |

| Floating-to-exact laundering | tiny residual called equality or infeasibility | no rational witness/dual certificate/exact check |
| Symmetry leakage | orbit reduction assumes a witness may be symmetrized without proof | reduced system lacks a transport theorem |

## 4. Control set

- **Known answer:** independently verify exact upstream MAX5–MAX10 certificates.
- **Sweep plant recovery:** hide a valid known certificate at the declared far edge of a candidate/symmetry census and require recovery.
- **Empty-region null:** run the unchanged checker on a deliberately impossible/empty registered slice and require no witness.
- **Census reconciliation:** accepted + rejected + skipped + failed equals generated, with orbit sizes and row/column counts reconciling.
- **Trivial-witness null:** zero/random/shape-correct corrupted certificates must be rejected; at least one algebra-preserving and one equality-destroying mutation.
- **Metamorphic invariance:** coordinate permutation and positive homogeneous scaling preserve a valid MAX witness; a coefficient perturbation that breaks a chamber identity must flip the verdict.

No computation may support a live claim until all six exact method families have evidence on the campaign subject.

## 5. Plausibility envelope

| Quantity | Tripwire |

### Detection floors

| Instrument | Detection floor / boundary | What a null may claim |
|---|---|---|
| floating LP/least squares | solver tolerances and conditioning recorded per run | no exact feasibility or infeasibility claim |
| exact restricted solver | only the fully enumerated registered ansatz/field/width | bounded null for that family only |
| finite point testing | only evaluated points/chambers | mismatch is decisive; agreement is not global equality |
| formal proof | exact encoded theorem and imports | nothing about informal target until statement match passes |

## 6. Identity and fixture containment

“Works only on the showcased case” means a checker, generator, or proof script depends on certificate ordering, file names, bundled fixtures, one coordinate labeling, or `n=10` constants. A generic mechanism must pass fresh relabelings/scalings, reject fresh corruptions, and either accept another independently generated valid instance or emit a typed refusal naming why it is instance-specific. MAX11 success may remain an isolated certificate; it must be labeled as such and cannot be described as the universal mechanism.
